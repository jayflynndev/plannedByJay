Keep agent contacts, portal, account reference and evidence private.
2. Confirm that New Travel works with this operator. Record the network checkbox, permission basis, confirmation date, actual restrictions/evidence and **Confirmed permission**. **Restricted / not permitted** retains the existing `declined` database state; pending/restricted/inactive suppliers cannot publish. The separate global launch gate remains unchanged.
3. Verify public disclosures against official information and record the source/date privately. For applicable ATOL arrangements, check the actual holder/name/number in the [CAA register](https://www.caa.co.uk/atol-protection/check-an-atol/search-atol-holders/), plus entitlement and applicable protection wording. ABTA P8763 is not an ATOL number. Enter authorised ATOL artwork as an existing `/images/...` asset path; an owner adds the approved file separately. No artwork or licence number is invented or downloaded by this feature.
4. Registry information is reused automatically on every linked offer. Editing a supplier invalidates linked offer versions/revisions: copy unsaved work and reload an already-open offer editor before saving. Previously captured enquiries and email snapshots do not change.

## Enter, check, preview

- Save partial drafts at any time. Inline supplier creation, failed saves, photographs, private notes and duplication retain the current offer. Duplicates require fresh verification, expiry and review.
- Specify whether flights are included and record **ATOL applicable / not applicable / pending**, with a supporting classification reason. Pending cannot publish. Fly-cruise requires flights and applicable verified ATOL details; cruise-only, hotel-only and rail require non-flight classification. Non-flight arrangements need no invented ATOL number. Where relevant, record separately verified product-specific non-flight protection in the offer; a supplier's ATOL wording is never automatically applied to a non-flight product. The initial implementation deliberately blocks flight-inclusive exceptions: refer them to New Travel for assessment rather than selecting not applicable. Changing the label alone cannot satisfy the server/database checks.
- Enter the full price in the selected currency and its per-person or total-party basis, party size, adults/children and precise occupancy/child ages where relevant. Counts must agree. Per-person pricing applies uniformly to that stated party; use total-party basis for mixed child/adult rates. The website also displays the calculated party price.
- Include every reasonably calculable mandatory tax, resort/local fee and unavoidable supplement in that price, even if payable locally. Explain pay-now/pay-locally splits and any currency conversion/rate in the included-charges field. A deposit is never the holiday price. Explicitly confirm the full-price check.
- Only where a charge genuinely cannot reasonably be calculated in advance, state why, its calculation method and enough information for the customer to calculate it. This is shown prominently next to the price on cards and details; it is not a general licence to omit known charges. Clearly separate optional extras.
- Record destination/accommodation, dates, departure airport/port/station where relevant, nights, board/cabin, verified main inclusions/exclusions, promotion conditions/expiry and relevant booking/cancellation rights or a verified explanation. Include deviations from normal payment/performance/complaint arrangements in the conditions. Do not use 'subject to availability' to replace material information.
- Check actual supplier price and availability, then record accurate UTC check times. Publication expiry must be future and within seven days of the price check. An earlier promotion expiry, past departure or stale verification hides the offer; saving does not refresh checks. This seven-day limit is our operational rule, not a statutory guarantee.
- Preview the saved offer on mobile and desktop. Check cards as advertisements in their own right: operator/legal identity, public address/contact, agency identity, applicable ATOL information, price/basis/party total, occupancy, dates/nights, main inclusions, important exclusions/conditions and charges must be readable. Detail pages add the full description, optional extras, official website and Booking Information link. Long conditions should be concise accurate summaries, not an internal compliance essay. Inspect actual artwork and its number, not merely its configured path.
- Review supplier promotion evidence and image rights, then explicitly review the offer. Publishing still requires the existing separate owner launch approval and supplier approval. This checklist does not authorise either gate or enquiry activation.

Retain the original supplier promotion/quote, date/time, source/reference, price/occupancy, taxes/local fees, inclusions/exclusions, promotion conditions, network/ATOL verification, permitted asset use and the version approved. Use private registry/offer notes and approved evidence storage; never upload confidential documents to the public media bucket.

## Basis for the website safeguards

These are distinct from the supplied vCRM workflow:

- **Wider advertising law:** [CMA207, invitations to purchase](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/unfair-commercial-practices-cma207/unfair-commercial-practices) covers priced advertising even without a purchase facility. Required information includes product characteristics, price, trader/represented-party identity and contact details, applicable cancellation rights and other legally required information. Presentation and medium limitations matter; a secondary link is not automatically sufficient. The implementation keeps these facts visible on cards as well as details.
- **Pricing:** [CMA209, updated January 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/price-transparency-cma209/providing-clear-and-accurate-information-about-prices-summary) requires upfront total pricing including calculable mandatory/local charges. Uncalculable charges need a usable calculation explanation. Our full-price confirmation and public disclosures support that requirement; software cannot verify the truth of a supplier promotion.
- **Trade guidance:** [ABTA Code guidance, February 2026](https://www.abta.com/sites/default/files/media/document/uploads/Code%20of%20Conduct%20Guidance%20Feb%202026.pdf), advertising/price indications and website standards, reinforces clear price and trader information. Use current CMA treatment for mandatory local fees; do not rely on older wording to omit calculable charges from the headline total. Membership duties and the legal requirements are distinct.
- **ATOL:** [CAA Standard Term 1 guidance](https://www.caa.co.uk/atol-protection/atol-compliance/complying-with-the-atol-standard-terms/compliance-with-atol-standard-term-1/) addresses holders and agents, publicity and pre-sale information, including holder identity, number, logo and appropriate protection statements. Actual applicability, artwork entitlement and the complete approved wording for this agency/product must be checked with New Travel. A registry entry alone does not prove every product is protected. This code displays recorded verified material; it does not certify legal compliance.
- **Good practice/implementation choices:** seven-day freshness, explicit review, immutable server snapshots and conservative blocking of unsupported flight exceptions are safeguards chosen for this website, not claims about express VTARSC or statutory requirements.

Remaining genuine release decisions are the individual supplier/product facts, licensed assets and applicable complete protection/booking wording, manual migration review, and the separately authorised global launch. General New Travel advertising permission is not an outstanding question.
