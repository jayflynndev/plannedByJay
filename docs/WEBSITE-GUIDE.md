# Planned by Jay website guide

The project contains the existing public website, Articles CMS, Holiday Types CMS, private supplier directory and Holiday Offers CMS. Shared website copy remains local; records are edited in Admin. See [ARTICLES.md](ARTICLES.md), [HOLIDAY-TYPES.md](HOLIDAY-TYPES.md), [SUPPLIERS.md](SUPPLIERS.md) and [HOLIDAY-OFFERS.md](HOLIDAY-OFFERS.md) for setup and editing. Jay has confirmed the public Privacy Policy and the complete live Holiday Offer enquiry journey. Jay has also confirmed that the bespoke Plan My Holiday enquiry journey works in production. The existing activation setting and enquiry services are unchanged. See [BESPOKE-ENQUIRIES.md](BESPOKE-ENQUIRIES.md). Bookings and payments remain absent.

## Edit the existing website

- `src/content/home.ts`: homepage wording, hero imagery and portrait cropping. Holiday cards come from published Holiday Types.
- `src/content/why-jay.ts`: personal introduction, portrait path/alt/object-position.
- `src/content/site.ts`: verified business/contact details, approved logos, footer and social links.
- `src/content/navigation.ts`: public navigation and canonical Holiday Brief action; RootLayout passes one snapshot to the client navbar.
- `src/content/holiday-types.ts`: shared Holiday Types listing labels only. Edit page names, introductions, headings, formatted content, photographs and closing invitations at `/admin/holiday-types`. The database is the sole source of page records; the original six are imported by the manual migration, with their URLs and deletion protected. FAQ material is removed.
- `/admin/articles`: published articles show **Save Changes**, drafts **Publish**. **Feature on Homepage** and **Pin to Top of Travel Advice** are independent; both respect publication and expiry. Paste basic tables from Word/ChatGPT and use the existing Table dropdown for rows, columns and header cells. Public tables scroll on narrow screens.
- `src/content/image-descriptions.ts`: corrections for specific reviewed image assets and their old placeholder labels. Owner-supplied replacement descriptions take precedence. No hosted image records are rewritten automatically.
- `src/lib/related-holidays.ts`: deliberate links between existing published Holiday Types. This does not match or recommend articles.
- `src/content/articles.ts` and `src/content/offers.ts`: public listing labels/empty-state copy. Manage Articles at `/admin/articles` and Holiday Offers at `/admin/holiday-offers`. Do not insert fictional or expired offers to fill the layout.
- `src/content/enquiry.ts`: existing Holiday Brief questions, labels and options. Keep stored option values stable. Validation/conditional rules are in `src/lib/enquiries/schema.ts`. The three stages support editing and review, private submission and a reference/confirmation after activation. No browser answer storage or marketing subscription is added.
- `src/content/policies.ts`: public policy copy and local approval guards. The approved Privacy Policy covers both enquiry routes under the established New Travel arrangement; its technical description now includes the connected bespoke form. No new responsibility/provider questionnaire is required. Cookies remains unpublished. Booking Information retains its existing text and approval. Publishing a policy does not enable bespoke submissions independently of their setup.
- `/admin/suppliers`: search existing private supplier records instantly, add/edit details and archive/restore with confirmation. Only the name is required. Draft permits incomplete records; Approved means Jay has reviewed the information. Enter or paste the details in one form, then Save Supplier or Save Changes. ATOL details are optional; email and contact-page URL are alternatives. Published offers reuse applicable supplier identity/contact/terms fields without exposing the supplier table itself.
- `/admin/offer-enquiries`: review securely stored offer enquiries and pending/unconfirmed email notifications. Retry the saved notification without recreating the enquiry; check older messages in Resend/the mailbox before resending. A Resend receipt means accepted by the service, not proof of mailbox arrival. See [OFFER-ENQUIRY-LAUNCH.md](OFFER-ENQUIRY-LAUNCH.md) for privacy approval, the owner-authorised delivery test and the existing launch flag.
- `/admin/bespoke-enquiries`: open original submitted briefs using View Details, read all saved answers and retry pending notifications through the same protected Resend recovery pattern. See [BESPOKE-ENQUIRIES.md](BESPOKE-ENQUIRIES.md) for the single migration and activation action.
- `/admin/holiday-offers`: select an active Approved supplier, enter holiday/travel/party-price details, choose existing photographs and published Holiday Types, optionally choose one sash and Featured on Homepage, then save/preview/publish. One record supplies the main catalogue, related Holiday Type pages and its own stable URL. Only explicitly featured eligible offers appear on the homepage. Archive retains IDs and enquiries; duplicates start as private drafts. A six-digit permanent offer reference is assigned automatically on first save and shown in the list/editor and public cards/detail pages; duplicates get a new number. No reference entry is needed. See HOLIDAY-OFFERS.md for manual migrations and reference reconciliation; enquiries remain operational.

Public images remain in `public/images/` or the shared article-images bucket; the portrait is reused, not duplicated. Brand/layout styles remain in `src/app/globals.css`, public article/offer styles in `src/app/content.css`, and the bespoke form layout in `src/app/enquiry.css`. Keep the current responsive design and British English. Draft, archived, expired and departed offers return 404; no archived records are served.

## Pages and checks

Home, Why Jay, Holiday Types (`/holidays`), Travel Advice, Holiday Offers, the existing Holiday Brief and approved policy routes remain. Published Holiday Types automatically appear on the landing page and homepage, with one shared `/holidays/[slug]` template and generated metadata. The original six stay in order; new types follow them. Draft/unpublished types return 404 publicly. Travel Advice displays published, unexpired articles, pinned first and then newest first. Homepage article cards use only articles explicitly marked Feature on Homepage. The sitemap reads published CMS routes and approved policies. `/robots.txt` allows public crawling, excludes Admin/API paths and links the sitemap. Root metadata defaults to noindex; for the final production launch set `SITE_INDEXABLE=true` alongside `SITE_URL=https://plannedbyjay.com` before building/deploying. Vercel previews stay noindex. Private/draft pages remain excluded. This indexing setting does not activate or gate enquiries.

Use Node 22.13+. `npm ci`, `npm test`, `npm run lint`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run build`. Both CMS sections reuse the publishable Supabase configuration in `.env.example`; no new environment variables are needed. `.env.local` and hosted credentials were not changed. Before releasing Holiday Types, manually run `supabase/migrations/20261026_holiday_types_cms.sql` in SQL Editor against the existing Articles project. Do not re-run the original Articles migration. The new seed is re-runnable and does not overwrite later edits.

Source/configuration edits require an owner-authorised deployment. No automatic push, deployment, database reset or future backend design is part of this work. Until deployed, production remains the previous release.

For Holiday Offers, manually run `supabase/migrations/20261026_holiday_offers_cms.sql` after the existing Articles, Holiday Types and Suppliers migrations. Then manually run `supabase/migrations/20261026_holiday_offer_references.sql` to backfill six-digit references. For this snagging release, existing installations instead need only the new `supabase/migrations/20261027_random_offer_references.sql` after that already-applied reference migration. Also run `supabase/migrations/20261027_article_placement.sql`. Apply both new migrations **before deploying** this release; no offers need recreating. Never rerun the old reference migration after the random correction. See [V1-SNAGGING.md](V1-SNAGGING.md). Offers need no new environment variables or dependencies. Their `deliveryVerified` constant remains true, and Jay has confirmed a successful public-form submission, saved record, inbox notification and acknowledgement. No offer workflow or activation change is part of bespoke setup.

For bespoke enquiries, manually run `supabase/migrations/20261027_bespoke_enquiries.sql`, set `BESPOKE_ENQUIRIES_ENABLED=true` in the existing deployment environment and deploy normally. Reuse the current private Supabase/Resend settings. Before this switch is set, the answer fields and submission endpoint remain closed. Test once using Jay's own details and check Admin, inbox and on-screen reference. No hosted migration, configuration change or live bespoke email was performed automatically.

The original bespoke setup is now applied and deployed, as confirmed by Jay. For the reference-format correction, **deploy the compatible application first**, then manually run `supabase/migrations/20261027_bespoke_enquiry_references.sql`. Do not edit or rerun the original migration. New enquiries receive random six-digit strings. Existing PBJ codes are retained as permanent customer aliases alongside their new numeric reference; original notifications and repeated confirmations keep their issued code. See [BESPOKE-ENQUIRIES.md](BESPOKE-ENQUIRIES.md#six-digit-reference-correction-application-order) for reconciliation and rollout details. No activation, security or Resend settings change.

For a fresh supplier-directory installation, manually run `supabase/migrations/20261026_suppliers_directory.sql` in Supabase SQL Editor. Existing installations need no migration for the manual-form cleanup. No extra credentials, services or environment variables are needed. Existing supplier records and permissions are preserved. See SUPPLIERS.md for adding, editing and archiving records.


Article editor polish: choose Paragraph, Heading or Subheading from the text-style dropdown; the compact formatting buttons have tooltips and accessible labels. Existing paste and keyboard shortcuts are retained. Beneath the title, **Short preview / introduction (optional)** accepts up to 200 characters (aim for 150-200). It supplies public article cards and SEO/social descriptions; leaving it blank generates a body-text preview without headings, preferring complete sentences. Existing stored previews remain available. No SQL migration is required: the existing excerpt column is reused.

Save Draft, Publish and Unpublish temporarily disable editing and show progress beside the controls. Results remain there until the next edit/action. Successful publication provides View Article and Copy Link using the saved slug; expired articles instead explain why they are not public. Failed saves keep the current editor content. Unpublish saves the current article as a private draft. No deployment or hosted database changes are performed by this update.

Polish validation: 21 automated tests passed (the public-route fixture used a temporary WebSocket shim for the installed Node 20 runtime). ESLint, TypeScript and production build passed. The project requires Node 22.13+; obtaining Node 22 locally was blocked by registry certificate verification. No browser connection was available for visual checks, and no hosted writes were tested.

Final snagging: two independent article placement controls require the small placement migration; published edits say Save Changes. Basic pasted tables are supported. Holiday Offer filters reset every control together, mobile cards put price/party before remaining facts, and ordinary text underlining is removed publicly. A duplicate leading article title is suppressed on display only.
