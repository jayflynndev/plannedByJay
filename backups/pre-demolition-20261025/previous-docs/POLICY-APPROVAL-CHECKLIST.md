## Lawful-basis reasoning and LIA for sign-off

This is a documented assessment proposal, not a signed legal determination. [ICO contract guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/a-guide-to-lawful-basis/contract/) limits contract to necessary steps requested by the person with the relevant controller. [ICO legitimate-interests guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/a-guide-to-lawful-basis/legitimate-interests/) requires purpose, necessity and balancing to be documented.

| Activity | Proposed basis and boundaries |
| --- | --- |
| Requested research and booking steps | Article 6(1)(b) only where necessary for that person's requested contract steps with the relevant controller. Otherwise assess 6(1)(f) for expected enquiry assistance; the agency relationship alone does not settle this. |
| Limited correspondence and unsuccessful-enquiry records | 6(1)(f) for continuity, answering questions and resolving issues, limited by the adopted review rule. No legal-obligation basis without identifying an actual duty. |
| Spam prevention, rate limiting and administrator security | 6(1)(f) for protecting customers, service availability and records; PECR exemption is a separate question. No automatic recognised-interest claim. |
| Later passenger/booking information | Assess with the responsible New Travel/supplier handler; do not blanket-apply the enquirer's contract basis to other passengers. Special-category data also needs a valid Article 9 condition in the separate approved workflow. |

**Purpose:** answer voluntarily requested enquiries, retain limited continuity and protect data/service access. No unrelated marketing, profiling for advertising or automatic VHUB transfer.

**Necessity/alternatives:** contact and core holiday requirements allow a useful response; optional details remain optional. Database/outbox durability prevents lost enquiries; snapshots make retries consistent. Assess whether retained fields, copies and 12-month period are proportionate versus shorter periods. Hashed rate buckets and limited auth access reduce exposure. Email-only processing would still create copies and does not itself replace secure records or abuse controls.

**Balancing:** customers expect an answer and reasonable protection, not indefinite retention. Child ages, free text, inferred accessibility needs and multiple traveller details can increase impact. Minimise collection, discourage medical/passport/payment details, restrict access, provide objection/email alternatives, use secure later collection, and apply holds only with specific justification. Assess residual risk from immutable snapshots, provider copies, transfers and long recovery-cookie persistence before sign-off. If safeguards do not address risks, revise processing or basis; do not approve merely to publish.

**Approval evidence:** responsible reviewer records the actual role/basis per activity, necessity findings, objections procedure, provider/erasure safeguards, any additional assessment required, name/date and review trigger in a restricted record. This proposal must not be mistaken for an approved LIA. Review on provider, purpose, collection or retention changes and after significant incidents.
